# CPU silicon identification

**CONFIRMED** AMD Custom CPU 1772 returns CPUID.1:EAX = 00A70FC0h (base family Fh + extended family Ah, model 7Ch, stepping 0). AMD explicitly maps this tuple to Hawkpoint2. Separate AMD entries identify Phoenix as 74h/1, Hawkpoint1 as 75h/2, and Phoenix2 as 78h/0. AMD's microcode appendix is a signature key, even though the installed microcode patch 0A70C005h predates the patch listed in the appendix. [AMD bulletin, appendix](https://www.amd.com/content/dam/amd/en/documents/resources/bulletin/technical-guidance-for-mitigating-transient-scheduler-attacks.pdf). [LLVM corroborating family ranges, commit fffc7fb7ad48d64d164565fdf54bec25267c9d22](https://llvm.googlesource.com/llvm-project/+/fffc7fb7ad48d64d164565fdf54bec25267c9d22/compiler-rt/lib/builtins/cpu_model/x86.c)

**STRONG EVIDENCE** for a compact Hawk Point 2 design related to Ryzen 5 8540U. Six active cores, 12 threads, one socket, one 16 MiB shared L3, 6 × 1 MiB L2 and 6 × 32 KiB each L1I and L1D. CPPC highest-performance values group physical cores 0 and 4 at 196 and the other four at 144, consistent with the 8540U’s two Zen 4 and four Zen 4c cores. Performance hints alone cannot identify core type. Extended topology leaf 80000026h supplies SMT, complex, CCD and socket levels but gives no distinct core-type labels on this host. AMD lists the 8540U at 137 mm² with Radeon 740M. Physical mask identity, package wiring, ordering part number, and the individual chip's die area remain unknown. No evidence of two disabled extra CPU cores. [AMD 8540U specifications](https://www.amd.com/en/products/processors/laptop/ryzen/8000-series/amd-ryzen-5-8540u.html)

Run scripts/inspect-cpu.sh. The private raw/cpu.txt contains per-thread leaf/subleaf register tuples, cache geometry, Linux flags and topology. Maximum standard and extended leaves are 10h and 80000028h. 80000008h EAX=3030h indicates 48-bit linear and physical addresses. 80000001h EBX=50000000h exposes package-type encoding 5. Its physical socket designation on Fremont is unverified. Advertised frequency policy maximum 4863.5352 MHz is a ceiling, not a measured sustained clock. The 30 W figure is Valve's product TDP. The 1022:14e8 root complex, Phoenix-associated 1022:15c7 security function and DMI HawkPoint corroborate the platform ancestry. None resolves a photomask revision beyond the published CPU stepping. [Valve specifications](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steammachine)

**Integrated graphics:** 07:00.0 is 1002:145a class 130000h, an unbound placeholder. The only graphics-class endpoint is the discrete 03:00.0. GamersNexus's firsthand engineering coverage identifies a fused-off IGP in its own narrative. With the commercial comparator this supports physical iGPU presence and product-level fusing. Individual fuse bits are UNKNOWN. No software enablement is established. The exact model of the disabled GPU cannot be read from the placeholder.
